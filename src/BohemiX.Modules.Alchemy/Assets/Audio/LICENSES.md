  https://freesound.org/people/liambratchford/sounds/785953/
- `water_drop.ogg`: "water drop 1.aif" by beskhu
  https://freesound.org/people/beskhu/sounds/273870/
- `mortar_grind.ogg`: "Marble herb grinder roll" by RossJuterbock
  https://freesound.org/people/RossJuterbock/sounds/543470/
- `bellows.ogg`: "bellows.wav" by lensson
  https://freesound.org/people/lensson/sounds/368498/
- `hourglass_sand.ogg`: "Hourglass - Sand flow light" by Vrymaa
  https://freesound.org/people/Vrymaa/sounds/825859/

The bundled files are the Freesound low-quality Ogg preview encodings of the
CC0 recordings linked above.
